# PRIVACY POLICY

**SwaraTuner**

**Last Updated: October 31, 2026**

## 1. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy describes how SwaraTuner ("the App") handles information when you download, install, or use the application.

SwaraTuner is developed and published by **Ninad Barve**, an independent individual developer based in India.

SwaraTuner is an offline musical reference tone application that generates precise reference frequencies to help users tune musical instruments and practise their voices. The App supports Carnatic, Hindustani, and Western music systems.

Your privacy is important to me. SwaraTuner is designed to operate entirely offline, without collecting or transmitting your personal information.

By using SwaraTuner, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

**We do not collect personal information through the App.**

SwaraTuner does not require users to create an account, register, log in, or provide any personal information to use its features.

The App does not collect:
- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers.
- Location information.
- Contacts or messages.
- Audio recordings or microphone input.
- Photographs or camera data.
- Payment or financial information.
- Browsing activity or usage analytics.

SwaraTuner does not use advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting services.

### Information stored locally on your device

The App may save certain preferences and settings to provide a consistent experience between sessions. These may include:

- Selected root note (Sa) for each music system.
- Reference pitch (A4 frequency).
- Fine-tuning (cents) and octave adjustments.
- Volume settings.

This information is stored exclusively in the App's private local storage on your device. It is not transmitted to me or uploaded to any external server. The App opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so these settings are not copied to your Google account or to another device.

The App does not require an internet connection to function.

## 3. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

Since SwaraTuner does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal information processing carried out through the App.

The App processes musical settings and generates reference frequencies locally on your device.

I do not use the information stored in the App for profiling, advertising, tracking, marketing, or any other external purpose.

### Communication through email

If you voluntarily contact me using the email address provided in this Privacy Policy, I will receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your communication.

This information will only be used to respond to your enquiry, provide support, or address privacy-related requests.

## 4. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION?

SwaraTuner does not sell, rent, trade, or share users' personal information with third parties.

The App does not transmit locally stored settings or other information to external servers.

I do not use third-party advertising networks, analytics services, or tracking technologies within the App.

If you contact me by email, your communication will necessarily be handled by your email service provider under its own privacy policy.

The App is distributed through Google Play. Google may independently process certain information in connection with the Play Store, app distribution, security, and its platform services. Such processing is governed by Google's own policies and is not performed by SwaraTuner.

## 5. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

SwaraTuner does not maintain a database of users or collect personal information through the App.

Settings and preferences saved by the App remain in its local storage on your device until they are cleared or removed.

You can remove locally stored App data through your Android device settings or by uninstalling the App.

If you contact me by email, your correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, resolve the matter, and meet any applicable legal obligations.

## 6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

SwaraTuner is a general-purpose musical utility application intended for users of all ages.

The App is not specifically directed at children under the age of 18 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults through its features.

No account registration or personal information is required to use the App.

If you believe that personal information belonging to a child has been provided to me through email, you may contact me at the address provided below.

## 7. PERMISSIONS AND DEVICE ACCESS

SwaraTuner does not require access to your device's sensitive features or personal information.

The App does not request or require permissions for:

- Microphone or audio recording.
- Camera or photographs.
- Location services.
- Contacts or messaging.
- Shared external storage.

The App generates reference tones directly and does not listen to, record, or analyse sounds through your device's microphone.

Settings are saved using the App's private local storage and do not require shared storage permissions.

## 8. SECURITY

SwaraTuner is designed to minimise the handling of personal information by keeping its functionality entirely offline.

Since the App does not transmit user settings or personal information to external servers, there is no user database maintained by me.

However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device.

## 9. UPDATES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, its functionality, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published at the same publicly accessible Privacy Policy URL, with the revised date displayed at the top.

You are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Continued use of the App after an updated policy is published constitutes acknowledgement of the revised policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## 10. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS AND DATA DELETION

Depending on the applicable laws in your country, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information concerning you.

Since SwaraTuner does not collect or maintain personal information through the App, there is generally no user account or remotely stored personal information for me to retrieve or delete.

You can clear all locally stored App settings and preferences through your Android device's application settings. Uninstalling the App will also remove its locally stored application data.

If you have contacted me by email and wish to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information contained in that correspondence, you can contact me using the email address below.

I will consider and respond to privacy-related requests in accordance with applicable data protection laws.
